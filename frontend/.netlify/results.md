SmoothScar now has a public portal for healthcare providers, a library of brochures to download, and three inquiry forms that save submissions through Netlify Forms. The consumer storefront works as before. The only changes to it are the new links in the header and footer.

**Provider portal (`/providers`).** The page opens with the "Partner With SmoothScar" hero and four buttons: Become a Provider Partner, Request Product Samples, Wholesale & Distribution Inquiry, and Download Clinical Resources. Below the hero are sections on why to offer BioRNA, the available products, the brochures, partnership, samples, wholesale, and how to contact the team. The wording avoids outcome guarantees, FDA claims and implied clinical endorsement. No wholesale prices are shown, and there are no provider accounts or logins.

**Brochure library (`/provider-resources`).** "BioRNA Professional Resources" lists the two official brochures from biorna.sg/brochure. Each has a thumbnail, title, description, and view and download buttons. The BioRNA Antiscar brochure links to the manufacturer's original PDF, and its download link makes the browser save the file. The manufacturer publishes the BioRNA-C brochure only as a ZIP, so that card says so clearly. It links to the official brochure page and to the official ZIP. Nothing was fabricated or replaced with screenshots. Permission to redistribute hasn't been confirmed, so the files are linked from the manufacturer's site rather than copied into this site. The confidential clinician deck isn't published.

**Inquiry forms (`/providers/partner`, `/providers/samples`, `/providers/wholesale`).** Each form has the required and optional fields from the brief and checks required fields, email and phone format, and US state codes. It requires a consent checkbox that links to a new `/privacy` page and asks people not to include patient or health information. The sample form says requests are reviewed and samples are not guaranteed. Each form is submitted to its own Netlify Forms entry (`provider-partner`, `sample-request`, `wholesale-inquiry`), so every lead shows which form it came from and when it was submitted. Spam protection comes from Netlify's Akismet filtering plus a honeypot field. The browser also enforces a one-minute wait between submissions. The confirmation message appears only after Netlify accepts the submission. A failed submission shows an error, keeps the answers, and offers a retry. A static copy of the forms in `public/__forms.html` lets Netlify find them during the build, and form detection was enabled for the site. Administrators can view and export leads as CSV under Forms in the Netlify dashboard. No credentials are stored in the site's code.

**Still required.** Form submissions and email notifications have not been tested yet; that requires the deployed preview. After the first deploy, check that the three forms appear under Forms in the Netlify dashboard and send one test submission per form. Then add an email notification for each form to your verified business address, which hasn't been provided yet. Netlify's limit on free form submissions applies, and going past it may require a paid add-on. Get written permission from RNAscence before hosting the brochure files locally.
