SmoothScar now has a clinical before-and-after gallery, "Real Patients. Documented Results." It appears on the homepage below the "How it works" section, on a new `/results` page, and on the BioRNA-C page, which shows only the C-section cases. The homepage and BioRNA-C versions include a "View Clinical Results" button that links to `/results`.

The reusable `BeforeAfterSlider` is a true overlay comparison. The AFTER photo fills the frame, and the BEFORE photo sits on top at exactly the same size and position, clipped at the divider. The divider starts at 50%. Dragging left shows more of the AFTER photo, and dragging right shows more of the BEFORE photo. It works with a mouse or touch, with the arrow, Home and End keys, and with screen-reader adjust actions. A circular sage handle with arrows sits on the divider, and BEFORE and AFTER labels sit on the photos. The frame keeps each photo's original proportions and fits photos rather than cropping them, so they are never stretched, cropped or edited. Visitors can filter by category (All, C-Section, Hypertrophic, Keloid, Post-Surgical) and choose a case. Each case shows its category, exact time interval, a factual description, the source page in the clinician deck, and the required results disclaimer.

All seven cases are set up in `src/clinicalResults.ts` in the requested order, with the time points from the brief: C-section p.17 (Day 0 vs. after 6 weeks), elbow p.8 (Day 0 vs. Day 22), wrist keloid p.11 (Day 0 vs. Day 7), C-section p.16 (Day 0 vs. Day 28), face p.9 (Day 0 vs. Day 16), knee keloid p.12 (Day 0 vs. Day 21) and shoulder p.10 (Day 0 vs. Day 6). The deck's text contains matching time-point labels.

**Photo authorization.** The clinician deck is marked confidential, and permission from the rights holder and patient consent haven't been confirmed. So no patient photos were extracted or published. Each case shows a clearly labeled placeholder ("Pending publication approval") that works with the slider. Six photos from this deck were already in the repository and were still being bundled into the live site through older code. They were removed from the repository and from the deployed site. They remain in the public GitHub repository's history. Once approval is in place, the steps for adding each case's original photos are documented at the top of `src/clinicalResults.ts`.

Type checks and lint passed for the changed files. The site wasn't built locally, and the slider hasn't been tested in a desktop or mobile browser yet. Netlify builds the site when this change deploys.
